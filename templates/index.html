SOURCES & LIMITATIONS

Transparent by design

Climate — NASA POWER monthly point series

2 m air temperature (T2M, aggregated to a day-weighted annual mean °C); corrected precipitation (PRECTOTCORR, monthly mean mm/day multiplied by days in month and summed to annual mm/year); relative humidity (RH2M, day-weighted % mean). A representative point per country is used, not an area-weighted national average.

Agriculture — World Bank WDI

Agricultural land (AG.LND.AGRI.ZS, % of land area) and cereal yield (AG.YLD.CREL.KG, kg/ha). Joined with NASA climate data on ISO3 country code and calendar year.

Model — Random Forest

Trained on available country-year rows; later years are held out for evaluation. Missing climate inputs are median-imputed for modeling only. Charts and summary metrics use actual observations.

Coverage and missing data

Only countries with retrieved and overlapping source observations are shown. Refresh results report source errors and missing required fields. No observations are fabricated or filled for display.
